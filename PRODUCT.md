# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Luxury residential and commercial clients: villa and apartment owners, property developers, and international buyers who commission high-end interior architecture and turnkey homes. Primary geography is Marbella / Costa del Sol, with international projects. They arrive seeking proof of taste, craft, and reliability before requesting an appointment or visiting the showroom.

## Product Purpose

Ambience Home Design’s marketing site presents Andrea Böck’s studio as a quiet-luxury interior architecture practice. Success means visitors feel calm confidence in the work, explore projects with clarity, and convert to a showroom visit, appointment request, or contact inquiry without friction or template-site noise.

## Brand Personality

Elegant, luxurious, modern, smooth, calming. Voice is precise and composed: material, place, and craft over hype. The interface should feel like walking into a well-lit showroom: spacious, ordered, and unhurried.

## Anti-references

- WordPress / Divi-style stacked sections, stock patterns, and cluttered carousels-as-chrome
- Generic multipurpose templates and “luxury hotel brochure” excess (gold flourishes, heavy ornaments)
- SaaS marketing tropes, icon-card grids, and loud conversion theater

## Design Principles

1. **Photography leads.** The rooms sell the brand; UI frames them, never competes.
2. **Quiet confidence.** Restraint in chrome and copy; one clear action per view: request a meeting.
3. **Material calm.** Soft contrast, generous air, and smooth ease-in/out motion that matches the interiors.
4. **Project storytelling.** Linked titles and images stay tied; every featured space has a clear path to its project.
5. **Showroom invitation.** Contact and meeting paths stay easy to find without shouting.
6. **No template noise.** No line separators, no small all-caps eyebrow titles, no springy motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain readable contrast on photography overlays, visible focus states, keyboard-operable navigation and carousel controls, and `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all motion.
